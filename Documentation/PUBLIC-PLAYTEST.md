Use Safari in landscape and tap Play. Den → Crimp opens the new trading counter. Bank two snacks for the first deal; trade once per banked outing. Three trades earn a permanent Golden Sardine decoration. Walk up to the counter to use Trade, or approach the wall-mounted clothes rail to open Wardrobe. Den → Wardrobe also remains available.

The starter bandana is available immediately; other looks require banked finds or outings. Wear it equips and saves; Coat changes fur color. The initial payload is about 122 MB. Saves belong to this browser/device and origin; cloud sync is not implemented. Existing URL and save identifiers are retained.
