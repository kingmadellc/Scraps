# Jimothy Survival 0.4.5

## Play

Mac: open `Builds/Jimothy.app` on the Jimothy Dev drive. Choose Continue to retain the existing native save.

iPhone/iPad: on the same Wi-Fi, open http://192.168.4.23:8765/?v=0.4.5 in Safari, keep the Mac awake and external drive connected, rotate to landscape, and tap Start. If an old session is open, reload it. Browser saves belong to that device/browser and are separate from the Mac save.

## New mechanics

Hostile NPC suspicion builds with proximity, facing and line of sight; the HUD shows the most suspicious nearby enemy. Break sight or gain distance to lose attention. NPCs investigate before chasing, search briefly after losing sight, and stay at ground level. Blocked humans turn and take a short supported rest; dogs pause with their own body language.

Jump onto the first amber access step, then keep moving along the treads. Use T, controller B/Circle, or the airborne touch Trick button to flip. Clean flips earn150 points, roof transfers100, and traveling chains can multiply the award. Every1,000 total style points pays5 shinies. High uninterrupted falls cause significant damage, unsafe tricks do not score, and airborne fast travel is blocked.
