# 0.3.2 — rear-follow camera and neighbor navigation

Camera heading now follows Jimothy’s heading, including while stopped or moving backward. A/D and horizontal thumb-pad input steer; W/S and vertical input drive forward/backward. Right mouse drag/right-side touch/right stick turn character and camera together and adjust pitch. This is a deliberate steering control scheme to satisfy the fixed rear-view request; it does not retain the previous free-orbit/sideways movement scheme.

Camera collision recovery searches elevation only, through 85 degrees in tight spaces. It retracts along that same rear heading. Distance is smoothed; camera position is not interpolated sideways around turns. Static building clearance is maintained; moving NPCs do not push the camera.

Neighbors now use a shared lazy 0.75m ground grid, capsule clearance, bounded A* searches, staggered replanning, no diagonal corner cuts, step-aware curb crossings, and local yielding to other controllers. Unreachable targets stop movement. Animation follows actual displacement. Contact damage also requires present line of sight, rather than threat memory alone. No new packages required. This does not claim every possible crowd configuration is resolved.

Validation: rear-camera audit passes actual motor forward/reverse, both steering directions, look-turn and jump, plus immediate/settled camera checks at 24 headings at each reported tree/wall location. All samples maintain rear alignment and tested static clearance. Neighbor audit passes actual CharacterController wall detour, 1.75m corridor, blocked target, and .20m curb ascent/descent. Initial neighbor fixture used render-dependent delta time; replaced with explicit 1/60s steps so geometry tests measure known simulated time. These are synthetic navigation fixtures, not a phone performance benchmark. Native visual verification is recorded in AgentPlaytests/v6-rear-camera.

Native handover: v6-rear-camera verified back view in forward, steering and reverse motion, plus elevated recovery by a tree/crate gap. v6-neighbors observed a fisherman walking along a storefront and a human crossing the curb into the road without obvious wall penetration in those frames. Both isolated test processes exited; no user-save load was used. The normal 0.3.2 app was reopened at its menu. Crowd deadlocks, every district route and iPhone performance remain outside these bounded checks.
