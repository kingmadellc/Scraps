# Ballard storefront identities

Ten original fictional identities use native geometry icons, custom wordmarks and architectural signs. The styles follow the district's modest masonry commercial character without copying existing merchant branding or asserting a surveyed reconstruction.

| Store | Mark / lettering | Sign treatment |
|---|---|---|
| The Net Loft | Anchor / Barlow Condensed | Navy pointed nautical board |
| Cedar & Salt | Cedar sprig / Pacifico | Painted timber with cream rules |
| Ballard Bakery | Scored loaf / Bree Serif | Cream oval, oxblood letters |
| The Last Cast | Fishing hook / Barlow Condensed | Wine board with brass-colored ink |
| Salmon & Sons | Salmon profile / Bree Serif | Navy swallow-ended fishmonger board |
| Copper Gull | Outstretched gull / Fugaz One | Warm terracotta fascia |

| Dockside Coffee | Cup and steam / Pacifico | Cream shaped painted board |
| Northwest Records | Grooved vinyl disc / Fugaz One | Navy record-store panel |

Upper-facade variation includes segmental brick arch surrounds, stone keystones, painted shutters, recessed parapet panels, modest pilasters and different cornice paint. These changes preserve roof levels, building footprints and tested climbing routes.

Research direction: Seattle's [Ballard neighborhood design guidelines](https://www.seattle.gov/documents/departments/sdci/about/ballarddg2019.pdf) describe varied storefront treatments and awnings/signs, including iconic pedestrian-scale hanging signage. The [Ballard Avenue Landmark District page](https://www.seattle.gov/neighborhoods/historic-preservation/historic-districts/ballard-avenue-landmark-district) identifies the district's governing guidelines. Direct full-page/PDF retrieval returned403 in this session, so this is a reference direction from indexed official excerpts, not a claim of full guideline review or compliance.

## Font provenance

Added Bree Serif and Pacifico from the official [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts), respectively `ofl/breeserif/BreeSerif-Regular.ttf` and `ofl/pacifico/Pacifico-Regular.ttf`. Their accompanying SIL Open Font License files are saved beside the fonts under `Unity/Assets/Jimothy/Resources/TitleFonts`. Existing Barlow Condensed and Fugaz One retain their existing OFL files. Icons and sign geometry are original code-generated assets, with no downloaded logo art.

`Jimothy.Editor.DistrictArtAudit.Run` produces ten shop closeups and two park/road views under `PlaytestCaptures/District`. Its camera poses use an isolated unsaved session. Rendering and image inspection must precede any final legibility claim.
