# Rigged human and dog replacement

`Tools/build_neighbors_v5.py` generates original native Blender models and exports `BallardHuman.fbx` / `BallardDog.fbx`, with Idle, Walk and Run. Source scenes live in `Art/Blender/BallardHuman.blend` and `BallardDog.blend`. Authored 1K cloth/coat atlases are stored alongside the FBXs; no external model or texture is bundled.

The human replaces the old cube jacket/limbs with a continuous tapered jacket, connected sleeves, pelvis/trousers, shaped shoes, fingers/thumbs, neck, jaw, nose, eyes/brows, ears and hair. It is an adult worker silhouette approximately 1.78m in rest stance (1.75m in relaxed idle), with faction jacket tint applied at runtime. The dog has a continuous ribcage/waist/neck/skull/muzzle, folded ears, tail, red collar/tag, articulated forelegs and knee/hock/paw rear chains. Its head reaches about .79m in idle, with body/withers around .65m in rest stance. These are authored stylized game assets, not scanned animals/people.

Human: 14,422 triangles / 15 bones. Dog: 16,260 triangles / 17 bones. Each exports as one skinned renderer with material regions. Rebuilding is deterministic.

Animation uses analytic limb placement, constant-velocity stance travel and lifted recovery. Human legs alternate with opposing arm swing and greater elbow flexion during running. Dog Walk staggers four contacts; Run is a fast diagonal trot with modest phase asymmetry. The first bounding experiment produced unacceptable hind-knee folding and was replaced before delivery. Torso weighting was corrected to keep the back/chest intact while limbs move.

Nominal in-place speeds: human Walk1m/s, Run3m/s; dog Walk.933333m/s, Run3.428571m/s. NeighborAI crossfades by actual measured movement and scales playback to that speed. When blocked or warning it settles to Idle rather than running in place. Pausing stops animation. Imported models replace dog/human visuals at true scale; existing cat/gull visuals remain. Their existing behavior is preserved. Fishing net follows the animated hand; jacket color preserves role distinction. Dog3.4m/s chase and .8m attack range, suspicion warning, kindness, home protection and cooldowns remain unchanged.

Before building Unity, call `Jimothy.Editor.NeighborAssetSetup.Prepare()` (menu: Jimothy / Prepare rigged neighbors). Its separate asset postprocessor ensures Generic animation/avatar import and loops, and Prepare creates/updates `BallardHumanController` and `BallardDogController` in Resources. Existing resource material importer retains the authored maps and converts to URP/Lit.

Validation: authored models rendered in native Blender Idle, Walk, Run and four quarter-cycle Run poses. All integer frames of all three clips checked: zero loop vertex error, zero foot orientation error, ankle gap below .000001m and no checked foot penetration. Lowest authored soles are at .016m human / .019m dog; runtime visual roots compensate those offsets to contact ground. Full numeric output is in `neighbors-v5.json` and `neighbors-v5-validation.json`. No Unity process was launched by this agent; final Unity import/shader/scene/playtest verification belongs to parent integration.
